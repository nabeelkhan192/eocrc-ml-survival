# Reporting checklists (complete before journal submission)

1. **TRIPOD+AI** — the 2024 reporting standard for clinical prediction models
   using machine learning. Collins GS, et al. BMJ 2024;385:e078378.
   Download the checklist from the EQUATOR Network (equator-network.org) or
   tripod-statement.org, complete every item with a manuscript page number,
   and submit it as a supplement. Journals in this space increasingly require it.

2. **STROBE** — for the observational-cohort aspects of the study
   (cohort selection, flow diagram, descriptive reporting).

3. **SEER acknowledgment** — include the required data acknowledgment text
   from your signed DUA in the manuscript's Acknowledgments section, and cite
   the exact database submission used (e.g., "SEER Research Data, 17 Registries,
   November 2024 Submission (2000-2022)").

Do not paste the checklists into this repo; keep completed copies in docs/
locally and submit with the manuscript.
